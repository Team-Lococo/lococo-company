## Why we started

As an exchange student in Spain, our founder saw interest in K-beauty growing fast across Europe — yet there was almost nowhere to test Korean skincare or get a trustworthy recommendation. The products you could find were expensive and took weeks to arrive.

So we set out to make great Korean skincare **easy to discover, easy to trust and fast to receive**, wherever you are in Europe.

## Mission

Korea has more than 30,000 beauty brands, but high overseas marketing costs and complex distribution keep most of them out of reach for European customers. Lococo is the bridge: we select brands, hold stock in Spain, build the creator and customer community, and give brands a real foothold in Europe.

> Our goal is simple: to become the place European customers go to for K-beauty — online and, from 2027, in our own stores — and the first partner Korean brands call when they look at Europe.

## What we believe

- **Honesty.** We share what works and what doesn't, with customers and with brands. Reviews on our platform are real.
- **Curation over volume.** Fewer products, chosen for skin goals and ingredients, explained clearly in Spanish and English.
- **Skincare is selfcare.** We don't sell perfect faces. We sell the small daily rituals that make you feel a little better every day.
- **Try the hard thing.** Every action defaults to failure, so we increase the number of attempts.

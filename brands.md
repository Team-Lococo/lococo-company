## What we offer

### 1. Retail and distribution in Spain
- Listing on **lococo.beauty**, our Spanish online store, with product pages in Spanish and English.
- **Stock held in Valencia** with our 3PL partner — delivery across Spain in 2–3 days, no customs surprises for the customer.
- Since July 2026 the EU charges duty on every small parcel from outside the EU. Holding stock in Spain is now the only way to stay competitive on price and speed.

### 2. Creator seeding and campaigns
- **400+ Spanish creators** in our K-Beauty Ambassador programme.
- Monthly seeding boxes, affiliate codes, giveaways and UGC you can reuse in your own channels.
- Honest reviews on our K-beauty review website, written by people who actually used the product.

### 3. Pop-ups and offline experience
- Pop-ups in Madrid with skin analysis and 1:1 routine consultations — sponsor slots for brands.
- Next: a three-week pop-up in Salamanca (Dec 2026) and our first flagship store in Madrid (2027).

### 4. Market feedback and reporting
- Sales, reviews and content performance in one report.
- What Spanish customers ask, what they compare you with, what they don't understand on your label.

In September 2026 we ran **Beauty Inside & Out** with Oki Doki Labs café in Madrid: a two-day event with AI skin analysis and creator content.

- Prepared for 150 guests, welcomed about **300**.
- **23 creators** produced **32 pieces of content** (Instagram and TikTok), reaching **127K+ views**.
- Earlier, our first pop-up **K-Beauty Skin Lab** (Dec 2025) brought six brands — VT, Arencia, TOCOBO, celimax, JUMISO and K-SECRET — to 400+ pre-registered visitors.
